Privacy Policy

📅 Last updated: 4 October 2026

👋 Introduction

Welcome to My Kennel: Dog Breeding Game, published by Palmer Digital Studio, the trading name of Mandy Palmer, a sole trader ("we", "us", or "our").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how information is handled when you use My Kennel, Kennel Community, Community Boards, Player Score, cloud save features, in-app purchases, support services and the MyKennelGame.com website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, why it is used, how it is stored and the choices available to you.

📦 Information We Collect

The information handled by My Kennel depends on which features you choose to use.

  • Gameplay information — including dogs, puppies, dog names, breeding and pregnancy information, pedigrees, genetics, game currencies, inventory, levels, XP, achievements, quests, show history, kennel progress, Player Score and game settings.
  • Kennel Community account information — including your email address, authenticated account identifier and Community player identifier.
  • Community profile information — including display name, kennel name, optional avatar and information you choose to make available through Kennel Community.
  • Community dog information — including dog identifiers, breeds, rarity, stars, levels, traits, genetics, pedigree information and other dog information used by public kennels, the Community Stud Book and Community breeding.
  • Community Board content — including topic titles, posts, replies, timestamps, edit information and moderation status.
  • Safety and moderation information — including blocks, reports, report reasons, optional report details and moderation records.
  • Device and activity information — including a generated installation or device identifier, platform, app version, first and last activity times and session count.
  • Purchase information — including Store product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase dates and information about rewards granted for completed purchases.
  • Support information — including your email address, message contents and any screenshots or attachments you choose to send when contacting us.
🛡️ My Kennel does not use your personal information for third-party advertising tracking and does not sell your personal information.

💾 Game Saves and Storage

Local game data

My Kennel stores normal game progress on your device. This can include your dogs, puppies, breeding history, currencies, inventory, progression, achievements, quests, dog-show results, kennel upgrades and settings.

Local backup and migration records may also be stored to help protect existing player progress when the game is updated.

Cloud saves

If you use supported online account features, My Kennel may also store a protected cloud copy of your game save to support account recovery and cross-device continuity.

A cloud save is linked to your authenticated account and can contain gameplay information such as your dogs, progression, currencies, inventory, breeding history and protected purchase records.

Cloud save information is stored using Supabase.

🐾 Kennel Community

Kennel Community is an optional online feature. Additional information is processed when you choose to create or use a Kennel Community account.

Account information

Kennel Community authentication uses an email address and password. Authentication and password-recovery services are provided using Supabase Auth.

Passwords are handled by the authentication service and are not displayed to Palmer Digital Studio.

Public Community profile

Your Community profile may include a Community player identifier, display name, kennel name and optional avatar.

Other signed-in Community players may be able to find your kennel through the Kennel Directory and view information you make available through Community features.

Community dogs

Dogs linked to Kennel Community may have a Community snapshot containing information such as:

  • dog name, breed and sex;
  • life stage and generation;
  • rarity, stars, evolution and dog level;
  • health, fertility, happiness, obedience, speed and charm;
  • bond level and traits;
  • coat colour and dog image;
  • rare genes and bloodlines;
  • crossbreed information; and
  • pedigree information about the dog's sire and dam where available.

Public Community views are designed not to expose your email address, password, game currencies or other private account information.

Community Stud Book and breeding

If you use Community breeding features, My Kennel may process Community dog identifiers, player identifiers, Stud Book listings, breeding requests, breeding transactions and litter records.

These records help operate breeding requests, prevent duplicate transactions and preserve cross-player breeding and pedigree history.

💬 Community Boards

Kennel Community includes public Community Boards for signed-in players. These are asynchronous discussion boards rather than private messaging or live chat.

If you use Community Boards, we process information including:

  • topic titles;
  • topic contents;
  • replies;
  • Community player identifiers;
  • creation and edit timestamps;
  • moderation status;
  • report information; and
  • blocking information where applicable.

Community Board content is used to provide discussions, operate content filtering and moderation, investigate reports, prevent abuse and help keep Kennel Community safe.

Players must accept the Community Rules before creating topics or replies.

Community Boards include content filtering, player reporting and blocking. Content may be hidden or removed where necessary.

🏆 Player Score and Weekly Competitions

My Kennel includes Player Score and weekly competitions.

When Player Score is used, we may process information relating to:

  • qualifying gameplay events;
  • Lifetime Player Score;
  • Weekly Player Score;
  • competition participation;
  • weekly rankings;
  • milestone claims;
  • competition results; and
  • rewards granted.

Player Score information is linked to your Community player identity so that your score can be preserved, duplicate awards can be prevented, rankings can be calculated and competition rewards can be granted.

📱 Device and Activity Information

When online account features are used, My Kennel may process a generated installation or device identifier together with information such as:

  • device platform;
  • app version;
  • first activity time;
  • last activity time; and
  • session count.

This information is used to operate the service, understand game usage, investigate technical issues, support analytics and protect player accounts and saved game data.

My Kennel does not use this identifier for third-party advertising tracking.

💳 In-App Purchases

My Kennel offers optional in-app purchases, including Coins, Gems and certain premium dogs or game items.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store or Google through Google Play. RevenueCat is used to load Store products, manage purchase status and support purchase restoration.

Palmer Digital Studio does not receive your full payment-card details from Apple or Google.

My Kennel keeps protected records of successfully fulfilled Store transactions so that paid rewards are not granted twice and purchases can be safely recovered.

These records may include:

  • Store product identifier;
  • Store transaction identifier;
  • purchase date;
  • type of purchase; and
  • the game reward granted.

Where a player uses an authenticated cloud save, protected purchase records may form part of the saved game data linked to that account.

Apple, Google and RevenueCat process purchase information under their own privacy policies and terms.

RevenueCat's Privacy Policy can be viewed at revenuecat.com/privacy .

  • In-app purchases are optional.
  • All prices are shown by the relevant Store before purchase.
  • Refunds are handled according to Apple or Google policies and applicable law.

🚫 Advertising and Tracking

My Kennel does not currently use player information for third-party advertising tracking.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

Device, usage, purchase and gameplay information described in this policy is used for app functionality, analytics, account protection, support and operation of the game.

👶 Children's Privacy

My Kennel is designed as a family-friendly dog breeding simulation game. Some optional online Community features involve account information and user-generated content.

Children should use online Community features only where permitted by applicable law and with appropriate parent or guardian involvement where required.

We do not knowingly use children's personal information for advertising tracking or sell personal information.

Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately can contact us at support@mykennelgame.com .

In-app purchases can also be restricted using Apple Screen Time, Family Sharing, Google Play parental controls and other device-level purchase settings.

🔗 Third-Party Services

My Kennel uses third-party services where necessary to operate the game and website.

  • Supabase — Kennel Community authentication, cloud saves, database services, Player Score and Community functionality.
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase products, purchase processing integration and purchase restoration.
  • Apple — App Store distribution and iOS in-app purchases.
  • Google — Google Play distribution and Android in-app purchases.
  • Cloudflare — website DNS, delivery, infrastructure and security.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies, terms and legal obligations.

⏳ How Long We Keep Information

Local gameplay data

Normal gameplay information remains stored locally on your device until it is removed through the game's supported reset process, app deletion or other device-level deletion of app data.

Cloud saves

Cloud save information may remain while your supported account and cloud save services are active, subject to account deletion and operational requirements.

Leaving Kennel Community

Leaving Kennel Community is different from permanently deleting the account.

Leaving may remove your public Community profile while certain account, Community player, kennel-link and Community dog information remains so that the same kennel can safely rejoin later.

Permanent Community account deletion

When supported permanent Community account deletion is completed, the authentication account and public Community profile are removed.

Community Board topics and replies authored by the deleted profile may have their authored text removed and be disconnected from the deleted player. Associated reports and Community-only relationship data may also be removed as part of the protected deletion process.

Some anonymous technical history may remain where necessary to preserve completed cross-player breeding, pedigree or transaction integrity.

Support records

Support communications are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate and respond to the request, maintain appropriate records, prevent abuse or meet legal obligations.
