Privacy Policy

📅 Last updated: 28 May 2026

👋 Introduction

Welcome to My Kennel, a dog breeding simulation game developed by Mandy Palmer ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using My Kennel, you agree to the practices described here.

📦 Information We Collect

My Kennel collects minimal data to provide you with the best game experience:

  • Game save data — your progress, dogs, coins, kennel name and player name are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage.
  • Purchase data — if you make in-app purchases, transactions are processed by Apple via the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage and verify purchases. RevenueCat may collect anonymised purchase identifiers.
  • Device identifiers — RevenueCat may collect an anonymous device identifier to associate purchases with your device. This identifier cannot be used to personally identify you.
🛡️ We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any other personal data.

💾 How Your Data Is Stored

All game progress is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your game data. If you delete the app, your local save data will be lost.

In-app purchase records are managed by Apple and RevenueCat. You can restore previous purchases at any time using the "Restore Purchases" button in the Shop.

💳 In-App Purchases

My Kennel offers optional in-app purchases for gems, which can be used to enhance your game experience. All purchases are processed securely by Apple.

We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to manage subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat's privacy policy can be found at revenuecat.com/privacy.

  • Purchases are non-refundable except as required by law.
  • In-app purchases are not required to play the game.
  • All prices are displayed in your local currency before purchase.

👶 Children's Privacy

My Kennel is a family-friendly game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The game does not include social features, chat, or the ability to share personal information.

In-app purchases require parental authorisation through Apple's Screen Time or Family Sharing features. We encourage parents to review their device purchase settings.

🔗 Third-Party Services

My Kennel uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple App Store — for app distribution and payment processing.
  • RevenueCat — for in-app purchase management and verification.

These services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their data practices. We encourage you to review their policies directly.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of My Kennel after changes to this policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.

⚖️ Your Rights

As all game data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can delete your save data at any time by uninstalling the app.

For any questions about data held by RevenueCat or Apple in relation to your purchases, please contact those services directly or contact us using the details below.
